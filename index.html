Explore America's National Parks

The United States National Park System includes 433 protected areas, featuring 63 designated national parks managed by the National Park Service.

Yosemite

1,200-square-mile protected area in California's Sierra Nevada mountains famous for its giant sequoias, waterfalls, and sheer granite cliffs.

Explore Yosemite >>

Zion

229-square miles preserve in Utah's first national park, celebrated globally for its massive cream, pink, and red Navajo sandstone cliffs and world-class slot canyons.

Explore Zion >>

Yellowstone

3,468-square-mile wilderness recreation area renowned for its incredible geothermal wonders and rich wildlife. The world's first ever national park.

Explore Yellowstone >>

Sequoia

631-square miles of a sprawling sanctuary in California's southern Sierra Nevada mountains renowned for protecting the world's largest ancient giant sequoia trees.

Explore Sequoia >>
